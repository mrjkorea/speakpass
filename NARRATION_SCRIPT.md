# Narration clips

Each id is one pre-recorded clip. Put the mp3 at `audio/narration/` using the file name below.
The app does not speak with a browser voice and does not use a second voice.
Until that mp3 is in place, the screen shows the text and the official preparation and response times still run.
Question 10 uses the same clip twice.

## cue.begin_preparing

File: `audio/narration/cue-begin_preparing.mp3`

Text: Begin preparing now.

## cue.begin_speaking

File: `audio/narration/cue-begin_speaking.mp3`

Text: Begin speaking now.

## directions.intro

File: `audio/narration/directions-intro.mp3`

Text: This speaking practice has 11 questions and takes about 20 minutes. You cannot pause or skip a question. The remaining time will appear on the screen.

## directions.read_aloud

File: `audio/narration/directions-read_aloud.mp3`

Text: A short text will appear. Use the preparation time to read it silently, including names, numbers, and any list. When the tone sounds, read the text aloud in a clear voice. Recording starts and stops by itself. You cannot pause.

## directions.describe_picture

File: `audio/narration/directions-describe_picture.mp3`

Text: A picture will appear. Use the preparation time to notice the place, the people, and what they are doing. When the tone sounds, describe the picture in detail. Recording starts and stops by itself. You cannot pause.

## directions.respond_questions

File: `audio/narration/directions-respond_questions.mp3`

Text: You will answer three questions about one everyday situation. The situation stays on the screen. Each question gives you a few seconds to prepare, then time to speak. Answer the first questions directly. On the last one, give reasons. Recording starts and stops by itself. You cannot pause.

## directions.info_questions

File: `audio/narration/directions-info_questions.mp3`

Text: You will have time to study a document quietly. Then you will answer three questions about it. The document stays on the screen while you answer. If a question includes a detail that does not match the document, correct it. On the last question, mention at least two items from the document. Recording starts and stops by itself. You cannot pause.

## directions.opinion

File: `audio/narration/directions-opinion.mp3`

Text: You will read one question about work, study, or daily life. Use the preparation time to choose a side and think of reasons and examples. When the tone sounds, explain your opinion clearly. Recording starts and stops by itself. You cannot pause.
